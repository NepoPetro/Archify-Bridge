﻿# Glossary

Term Definition
Tmura Hebrew for "bridge". The current repository, designed to convert Archify JSON IR into draw.io XML and static HTML.
Imut Hebrew for "identity" or "pattern". The parent project (1DFlfRMH) focused on authentication, digital rights, and governance.
JSON IR Intermediate Representation. The typed JSON schema used by Archify as the single source of truth for diagrams.
DAFO Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats (Spanish equivalent of SWOT analysis). Used in ADR-009 to evaluate Archify.
ADR Architecture Decision Record. A document capturing a significant architectural choice, its context, and its consequences.
IaC Infrastructure as Code. The practice of managing infrastructure through machine-readable definition files.
PoC Proof of Concept. A realization of a certain method or idea to demonstrate its feasibility.
