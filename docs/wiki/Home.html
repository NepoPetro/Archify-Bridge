﻿# Welcome to the Tmura Wiki

Tmura (Hebrew for "bridge") is a satellite project of Imut (1DFlfRMH), a data governance and digital rights platform.

This repository was created to solve a specific engineering challenge: producing reliable, verifiable, and aesthetically professional data lineage diagrams without compromising architectural density.

Purpose of this Wiki

This Wiki provides the conceptual and operational context that complements the technical codebase and the Architecture Decision Records (ADRs). Here you will find:

  • Glossary: Definitions of the specific terminology used across the project.
  • Architecture Context: The business and governance reasons behind Tmura.
  • Onboarding Guide: How to set up the environment and run the pipeline.
  • Evidence Standard: How Mathematica is used for formal proofs and PoCs.
